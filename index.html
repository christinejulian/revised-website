THE FAST-FOOD FAST-FLATION PHENOMENON: WHY GAMERS TURNED SATIRISTS

Published August 2026 | By QuestLogs Editorial Team | 6 Min Read

Over the past decade, fast-food prices across major chains like Burger King, Starbucks, Dunkin', and McDonald's have dramatically outstripped headline inflation rates. What was once budget-friendly convenience dining has increasingly evolved into a luxury, sparking widespread commentary and interactive satirical simulations.

From Dollar Menus to $18 Value Combos

Economic data reveals that menu items at major fast-food franchises have risen by over 100% since 2014. Factors frequently cited by executives include rising labor wages, supply chain bottlenecks, and agricultural costs. However, critics point to record gross margins as evidence of corporate price strategy.

"When a basic fast-food meal costs as much as a high-end mechanical gaming keyboard over a few weeks of dining, gamers realize something is broken in consumer economics."

Interactive Satire as Commentary

Building upon the legacy of Molleindustria's famous 2006 flash game McDonald's Video Game, web-based simulators place players directly in the role of corporate executives balancing rainforest deforestation, hormone usage, price hikes, and PR spin.
